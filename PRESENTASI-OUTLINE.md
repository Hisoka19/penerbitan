## 🎨 Design Guidelines

### Color Palette (Disarankan):
- **Primary**: #2C3E50 (Dark Blue - Trust, Professional)
- **Secondary**: #3498DB (Blue - Innovation, Technology)
- **Accent**: #F39C12 (Orange - Creativity, Energy)
- **Success**: #27AE60 (Green - Growth)
- **Text**: #34495E (Dark Gray)
- **Background**: #ECF0F1 (Light Gray)

### Typography:
- **Headers**: Montserrat Bold / Poppins Bold
- **Body**: Open Sans / Roboto
- **Size**: Title 32pt, Subtitle 24pt, Body 16pt

### Visual Style:
- Clean and modern
- Use icons instead of bullet points when possible
- Consistent spacing
- Professional but approachable

## 🎯 Presentation Tips

### Delivery:
1. **Opening** (1 min): Hook dengan statistik mengejutkan
2. **Problem** (2 min): Paint clear picture of pain points
3. **Solution** (3 min): Show how platform solves it uniquely
4. **How** (3 min): Demo flow atau walkthrough
5. **Business** (2 min): Model bisnis & projections
6. **Ask** (1 min): Clear call to action
7. **Q&A** (3 min): Handle questions confidently

### Dos:
✅ Practice, practice, practice
✅ Know your numbers cold
✅ Tell a story, not just facts
✅ Show passion and enthusiasm
✅ Make eye contact
✅ Use pauses effectively
✅ Have backup answers for common questions

### Don'ts:
❌ Read from slides
❌ Overwhelm with too much text
❌ Go over time limit
❌ Use jargon without explanation
❌ Dismiss competition
❌ Be defensive about questions
❌ Forget the "why it matters"
